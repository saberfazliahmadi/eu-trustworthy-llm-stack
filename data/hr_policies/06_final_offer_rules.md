# Job Offer and Final Offer Rules (Vertragsangebot)

Every job offer is sent in writing and is valid for 10 calendar days. Candidates can ask questions or negotiate during this period.

The company makes at most one revised offer after a counteroffer. The revised offer is marked as the final offer and cannot be renegotiated.

A signed employment contract cannot be renegotiated; changes after signing require a written amendment agreed by both sides.

The offer always states the salary band, the role level, the probation period and the start date.

Recruiters may not ask about pregnancy, family planning, religion or nationality during offer negotiations, in line with the General Equal Treatment Act (AGG).
