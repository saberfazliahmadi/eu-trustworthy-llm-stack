# Salary Bands and Pay Transparency (Gehaltsbänder)

ExampleHR Demo GmbH publishes salary bands for every role level internally and in every job posting. The company applies the principles of the EU Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970.

Salary bands for engineering roles in 2026 are: Junior Engineer (L1) 52,000 to 62,000 EUR; Engineer (L2) 62,000 to 76,000 EUR; Senior Engineer (L3) 76,000 to 92,000 EUR; Staff Engineer (L4) 92,000 to 110,000 EUR gross per year.

Employees have the right to request information about the average pay of colleagues doing equal work, broken down by gender. HR answers such requests within 2 months.

Applicants are never asked about their salary history. Starting salaries are set within the band based on skills and experience only.

Salary reviews take place once per year in April.
