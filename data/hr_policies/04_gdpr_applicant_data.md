# Applicant Data Protection (Datenschutz im Bewerbungsprozess)

Applicant data is processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and section 26 of the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

Applicant data is deleted 6 months after the application process ends, unless the applicant consents to a talent pool. Talent pool consent is valid for 24 months and can be withdrawn at any time.

Applicants have the right to access their data under Art. 15 GDPR and to request erasure under Art. 17 GDPR. Requests are answered within 1 month.

AI tools may support CV pre-screening, but no applicant is rejected solely by an automated decision (Art. 22 GDPR). A trained recruiter reviews every AI recommendation before a decision is made.

The data protection officer can be reached at datenschutz@nordlicht-digital.example.
