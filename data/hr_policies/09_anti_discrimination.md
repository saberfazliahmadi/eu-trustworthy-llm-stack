# Equal Treatment and Complaints (AGG-Beschwerdestelle)

Nordlicht Digital GmbH does not tolerate discrimination based on ethnic origin, gender, religion or belief, disability, age or sexual identity, as defined in the General Equal Treatment Act (AGG).

Employees and applicants can file a discrimination complaint with the internal AGG complaints office (Beschwerdestelle). Complaints are handled confidentially and receive a first response within 5 working days.

Applicants who believe they were discriminated against must assert claims in writing within 2 months of the rejection (section 15(4) AGG).

Harassment cases are always reviewed by a trained human case manager and are never decided by an automated system.

Employees can also contact the Federal Anti-Discrimination Agency (Antidiskriminierungsstelle des Bundes) for independent advice.
