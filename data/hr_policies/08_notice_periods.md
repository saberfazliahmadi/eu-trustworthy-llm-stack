# Notice Periods and Termination (Kündigungsfristen)

After the probation period, the notice period for employees is 3 months to the end of a calendar month.

For terminations by the employer, the statutory notice periods of section 622 BGB apply if they are longer: 4 months after 10 years of service, 5 months after 12 years, 6 months after 15 years and 7 months after 20 years.

Every termination must be made in writing with a handwritten signature (section 623 BGB). Termination by e-mail is invalid.

Before any termination by the employer, the works council must be consulted (section 102 BetrVG). A termination without consulting the works council is invalid.

Employees who want to challenge a termination must file a claim with the labour court within 3 weeks (section 4 KSchG).
