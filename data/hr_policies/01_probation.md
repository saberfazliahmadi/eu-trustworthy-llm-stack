# Probation Period (Probezeit)

All new permanent employees at Nordlicht Digital GmbH have a probation period of 6 months. The probation period cannot be extended beyond 6 months, in line with section 622(3) of the German Civil Code (BGB).

During the probation period, either side may terminate the employment contract with a notice period of 2 weeks. The termination must be made in writing with a handwritten signature; notice by e-mail or messenger is not valid.

A probation review meeting takes place in month 4 between the employee and their line manager. The outcome of the review is documented and shared with the employee.

Vacation entitlement accrues during probation at 2.5 days per full month of employment.
