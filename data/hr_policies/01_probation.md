# Probation Period (Probezeit)

All new permanent employees at ExampleHR Demo GmbH have a probation period of 6 months. The probation period cannot be extended beyond 6 months.

During the probation period, either side may terminate the employment contract with a notice period of 2 weeks; this shortened notice period applies during an agreed probation period of up to 6 months under section 622(3) of the German Civil Code (BGB). The termination must be made in writing with a handwritten signature; notice by e-mail or messenger is not valid.

A probation review meeting takes place in month 4 between the employee and their line manager. The outcome of the review is documented and shared with the employee.

Vacation entitlement accrues during probation at 2.5 days per full month of employment.
