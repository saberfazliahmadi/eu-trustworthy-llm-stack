# Remote Work and Home Office (Mobile Arbeit)

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week in agreement with their team. The works agreement on mobile work was concluded with the works council (Betriebsrat).

Remote work from another EU country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year and must be registered in the HR portal in advance because of social security rules (A1 certificate).

The company pays a home-office allowance of 30 EUR per month and provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset.

Working time rules of the Working Hours Act (ArbZG) also apply at home: a maximum of 10 hours per day and a rest period of at least 11 hours between two working days.
