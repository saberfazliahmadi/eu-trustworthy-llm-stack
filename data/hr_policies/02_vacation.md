# Vacation Policy (Urlaub)

Full-time employees working a 5-day week receive 30 vacation days per calendar year. The statutory minimum under the Federal Vacation Act (BUrlG) is 20 days for a 5-day week; the additional 10 days are contractual.

Part-time employees receive vacation days pro rata to their working days per week.

Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year and must be taken by 31 March. The company reminds employees in writing before vacation days expire; without this reminder, statutory vacation days do not lapse.

Vacation requests should be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance in the HR portal. Requests of more than 15 consecutive working days require approval by the department head.
