### Diagnostic report: the 4 known failure modes of reweighting

| failure mode | measurement | finding |
|---|---|---|
| 1. tokenizer fragmentation | tokens/surname german=1.45, international=7.00 | with token-level loss the minority share becomes 0.586 instead of 0.500 |
| 2. pretraining bias | frozen-embedding association before=0.286, after=0.286 | reweighting the head leaves the pretrained association unchanged |
| 3. label-noise amplification | share of loss weight on noisy labels 0.030 -> 0.125 | minority clean-test accuracy with 25% noisy minority labels: 0.559 (unweighted) vs 0.589 (reweighted) |
| 4. biased audit metric | true worst-group acc=0.698, proxy-inferred groups=0.704 | a noisy group proxy distorts the very metric used to certify fairness |
